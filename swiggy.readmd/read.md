# 🌟 Swiggy LifeOS - Intent-Driven Commerce Engine 🌟

## 📊 Project Status: Concept & Architecture Phase

---

### 🎯 What is Swiggy IntentOS?

**Swiggy IntentOS** (working title) is an **AI-native intent and life-events engine** designed to sit on top of:
- 🍕 **Swiggy Food**
- 🛒 **Instamart**
- 🍽️ **Dineout**

### 💡 Core Observation

Users do not think in terms of:
- ❌ Restaurants
- ❌ Grocery categories
- ❌ Coupons
- ❌ Reservations

**They think in terms of real-life situations:**
- 😋 Being hungry
- 📚 Studying for exams
- 🎉 Hosting friends
- 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Planning family dinner
- 💰 Managing tight budget
- 📦 Restocking groceries
- 🍽️ Choosing where to dine

Users are required to manually:
- 🔍 Search
- ⚖️ Compare
- 🎯 Filter
- 🤝 Coordinate
- 🧠 Decide

**This creates:**
- 😫 Decision fatigue

## 🌈 Our Vision

**Swiggy LifeOS** is an AI-powered decision and orchestration layer built on top of Swiggy's MCP ecosystem.
